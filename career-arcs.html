Career Arcs

Alexander Volkanovski's career arc — from a title loss that looked like the end of an era to a win over an undefeated contender that put him alone atop the UFC featherweight record book.

Spotlight

Career arc: Alexander Volkanovski

From a five-round rematch win over Max Holloway to a 38-year-old champion tying Jose Aldo's defense record — mapped title fight by title fight.

2016–2019 — The Rugby Convert

An unassuming start

Debuted in the UFC after a background in rugby league, building a perfect early Octagon run largely outside the spotlight.

Dec 2019 — The Upset

Dethroning Max Holloway

Won a unanimous decision over Max Holloway at UFC 245 to claim the UFC featherweight title, a result few saw coming.

2020–2023 — The Reign

Six defenses, two pound-for-pound runs

Turned back Holloway again, then Chan Sung Jung and Yair Rodriguez, holding the UFC's top pound-for-pound ranking across two separate stretches.

Feb 2024 — The Setback

Losing the belt to Ilia Topuria

Dropped the title to Ilia Topuria at UFC 298 — a result that briefly read like the close of a historic run.

2025–Oct 2026 — Rewriting The Record Book

Diego Lopes twice, then Movsar Evloev

Won the vacant title back from Diego Lopes, defended it against him again, then stopped a previously-undefeated Evloev at UFC 333 — his ninth title-fight win, breaking his tie with Jose Aldo, and his seventh defense, matching Aldo's divisional record.
