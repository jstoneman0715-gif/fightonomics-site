Spotlight

Career arc: Raul Rosas Jr.

From a Contender Series win at 17 to headlining a UFC card down on every scorecard — four years, mapped fight by fight.

Sep 2022 — Contender Series

Youngest UFC signee ever

Defeated Mando Gutierrez at 17 years, 11 months old to become the youngest athlete ever signed to the UFC.

Dec 2022 — UFC Debut

Youngest fighter to win a UFC bout

Submitted Jay Perrin at UFC 282, becoming the youngest person ever to win inside the Octagon.

Apr 2023 — First Loss

A gut check early

Dropped a unanimous decision to Christian Rodriguez at UFC 287 — his only career defeat.

2023–2026 — The Rebuild

Five straight wins

Rattled off finishes and decisions over Terrence Mitchell, Ricky Turcios, Aoriqileng, and Vince Morales, then closed the run with a decision over former title challenger Rob Font at UFC 326.

Sep 2026 — UFC Vegas 121

Down and out, then a first main-event win

Trailed on all three scorecards through four rounds before stopping Raoni Barcelos in round five, extending his streak to six.
