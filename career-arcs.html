Spotlight

Career arc: Salahdine Parnasse

From an 11-year-old at Atch Academy to a two-division champion abroad — and now a first-round knockout in his own backyard.

Age 11 — The Foundation

Started at Atch Academy

Began training under Stéphane Chaufourier as a child — the same gym and coach behind his entire career since.

2019–2022 — Two-Division Reign

Four KSW titles

Won the interim featherweight title in 2019, the featherweight title in 2021, and the interim lightweight title in 2022 for Poland's KSW promotion.

2024 — Undisputed Champion

Unified the lightweight title

Claimed the KSW lightweight championship outright and defended it four times, building a 23-2 record along the way.

Sep 2026 — UFC Debut

A rare main-event assignment

Signed with the UFC and was handed a main event slot in his home country against a ranked veteran — an unusually high-profile debut.

Sep 2026 — UFC Paris

Delivered immediately

Knocked out Dan Hooker at 2:35 of round one in Paris, improved to 24-2, and called out Max Holloway.
