Natalia Silva's career arc — from early losses on the regional circuit to a 14-fight win streak, three wins over former champions, and her first UFC title.

Spotlight

Career arc: Natalia Silva

From a 20-5-1 record built the hard way to the UFC's newest champion — mapped fight by fight.

Jun 2022 — UFC Debut

Opened her UFC run

Won a wide decision over Jasmine Jasudavicius to begin what became a 14-fight winning streak.

2022–2023 — Building Momentum

Finishes and ranked wins

Rattled off back-to-back finishes over Tereza Bleda and Victoria Leonardo, then added decisions over Andrea Lee and Viviane Araujo.

2024–2025 — The Statement Run

Three former champions, three wins

Beat Jéssica Andrade, Alexa Grasso, and Rose Namajunas in succession, establishing herself as the division's No. 1 contender.

Sep 2026 — A Title Shot Opens Up

Shevchenko vacates

Champion Valentina Shevchenko withdrew from a planned title defense and vacated the belt due to an injury expected to sideline her a year, clearing Silva's path to the vacant title.

Oct 2026 — UFC 332

First UFC title

Outpointed Wang Cong over five rounds to capture the vacant women's flyweight championship, improving to 9-0 in the UFC.
