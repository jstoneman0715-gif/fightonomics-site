Career arc: Brendan Allen

From a pair of five-round regional decision losses to a 14-finish submission résumé — mapped fight by fight.

2016–2018 — Regional Setbacks

Two five-round losses

Dropped decisions to Eryk Anders and Anthony Hernandez on the regional scene, sandwiched around winning the LFA middleweight title.

Oct 2019 — UFC Debut

Submitted Kevin Holland

Opened his Octagon run with a rear-naked choke — the first of fourteen career finishes by that method.

2020 — A Reality Check

TKO loss to Sean Strickland

A setback that preceded a seven-fight win streak through the middleweight rankings.

Late 2024–Early 2025 — A Rough Patch

Back-to-back decision losses

Dropped consecutive decisions to Nassourdine Imavov and Anthony Hernandez — his second career loss to Hernandez.

2025–2026 — The Rebuild

Four straight wins

Beat Marvin Vettori, Reinier de Ridder, and Edmen Shahbazyan, then submitted Christian Leroy Duncan in round five — a run that's put him a win away from title contention.
