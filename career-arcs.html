Career arc: Mike Malott

From a regional knockout title and a Contender Series submission to a UFC résumé that now includes Kevin Holland and Gilbert Burns — mapped fight by fight.

2011–2014 — The Regional Climb

A title after a loss

Turned pro with a first-round submission, then rebounded from a loss to Hakeem Dawodu by winning the vacant XFFC Lightweight title in 36 seconds.

Contender Series — The Breakthrough

Earned a UFC contract

Submitted Shimon Smotritsky on Season 5 of Dana White's Contender Series to earn his shot.

Apr 2022 — Jan 2024 — Learning The Division

Finishes, then a setback

Opened his UFC run with a TKO of Mickey Gall and submissions of Yohan Lainesse and Adam Fugitt, before a TKO loss to Neil Magny.

2024–2025 — The Streak Begins

Four straight wins

Decisioned Trevin Giles, knocked out Charles Radtke, then scored the biggest win of his career to that point over veteran Kevin Holland.

Apr–Oct 2026 — Contender Form

Gilbert Burns, then Joaquin Buckley

Stopped Gilbert Burns by strikes, then flattened Joaquin Buckley with a knee in front of his home crowd in Edmonton — five straight, and a move toward the top 10.
