Career arc: Alexandre Pantoja

From an eight-fight win streak and four title defenses to a freak-injury loss and a decisive rematch defeat — mapped fight by fight.

Jul 2023 — UFC 290

Won the flyweight title

Defeated Brandon Moreno to capture the UFC flyweight championship, launching an eight-fight win streak.

2023–2025 — The Reign

Four consecutive defenses

Built a case as the greatest flyweight ever, setting UFC divisional records for most wins, most submissions, and most finishes.

Dec 2025 — UFC 323

A freak ending

Lost the title to 24-year-old Joshua Van just 26 seconds in, after suffering an arm injury on a caught kick — a result that settled nothing on merit.

Sep 2026 — UFC 331

A second straight loss

Dropped a clear unanimous decision to Van in the rematch, falling to 30-7 and putting his path back to the title in real doubt.
