Spotlight

Career arc: Gregory Rodrigues

From a first-round TKO loss in his pro debut to out-pointing a hometown favorite in Sacramento — twelve years, mapped fight by fight.

2014 — Pro Debut

Lost in the first fight

Dropped his professional debut by first-round TKO to Bruno Lopes at Jungle Fight 73 — one of six losses on the road to tonight's record.

2019–2021 — The Climb

A regional-circuit gauntlet

Scored a body-kick knockout on the way up, absorbed a Contender Series knockout loss, then closed out back-to-back LFA finishes to finally earn a UFC contract.

2021 — UFC Debut

Opened his UFC run

Outpointed Duško Todorović in his promotional debut, then knocked out Jun Yong Park later that year.

UFC 322 & UFC 326 — Statement Wins

Avenged an old loss

Outpointed Roman Kopylov at UFC 322, then closed the loop with a first-round knockout rematch win over Brunno Ferreira at UFC 326.

Aug 2026 — UFC Sacramento

Beat the hometown favorite

Outpointed Anthony Hernandez over five rounds for the biggest win of his career, extending his streak to four straight and calling out Dricus du Plessis.
