Career Arcs

Tom Nolan's career arc — from a first-round knockout that earned him a UFC contract on the spot, through a brutal first-round loss in his own debut, to his first main event and his biggest win yet.

Spotlight

Career arc: Tom Nolan

From a Contender Series knockout that Dana White called a "straight killer" performance to a plus-money underdog who got off the canvas and finished a UFC veteran — mapped fight by fight.

2021–2023 — The Regional Run

Built in Brisbane, unbeaten in Australia

Turned pro and ran his record up fighting for Eternal Fighting Championship, training out of the Team Compton Training Centre in Brisbane.

Aug 2023 — The Breakthrough

A one-night UFC contract

Knocked out Bogdan Grad in the first round on Dana White's Contender Series Season 7; White called him a "straight killer" and signed him on the spot.

Jan 2024 — The Setback

Stopped in his own UFC debut

Lost to Nikolas Motta via first-round strikes at 1:03 — a fast, humbling introduction to the deep end of the UFC lightweight division.

May 2024 – Sep 2025 — The Rebuild

Four straight wins

Answered the loss by stopping Victor Martinez, then collecting decisions over Alex Reyes and Viacheslav Borshchev before beating Charlie Campbell — climbing into the rankings fight by fight.

Oct 2026 — The First Main Event

An underdog line, a signature win

Stepped in as a late addition to headline UFC Vegas 123 against veteran Renato Moicano, closed as a betting underdog, and finished him with strikes in round three for the biggest win of his career.
