Fightonomics is built on more than spreadsheets — it comes from time spent in the ring and on the mat. As an amateur boxer, I climbed all the way to a state championship, learning firsthand how reach, footwork, and fight IQ actually translate to results. That same drive carried over to wrestling, where I also won a state title and learned to read control, positioning, and pace the way judges never quite manage to. For the last fifteen years, I've watched nearly every major MMA fight, studying the sport from prelim cards to pay-per-view main events. This site is where that competitive background meets the numbers — breaking fights down the way someone who's actually competed sees them.
